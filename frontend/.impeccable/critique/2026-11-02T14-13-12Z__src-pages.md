## Design Specificity Verdict
About 60% authored. Specific to this product: the paper and ink, the mono case IDs, the login panel, the stage-tone system, and the case pipeline. Generic SaaS: KPI tiles, the stat-card summary, and data tables. Biggest miss: the evidence itself has no designed form. Officers see the detection result through `.debug-card`, which uses generic monospace (Courier New on Windows), in DetectionPanel.tsx.

Deterministic scan: 137 findings (133 advisory, 4 warning). There are 79 font-size and 50 radius advisories, measured against the new DESIGN.md, whose type and radius scales are too narrow, so these are mostly a documentation gap. The 4 side-tab warnings are intentional stage and error edges. There are 4 undocumented rgba tints. The overlay was injected only in a headless test browser, so no user-visible overlay.

## Priority Issues
1. [P1] The detection result is a debug dump. Officers see it in Courier text, with bracketed severities and native buttons, below the extracted fields. The LangGraph and case analysis traces sit open in the main flow. Fix: a verdict header, finding rows with the existing severity tags, a link from each finding to its place in the document, the result shown above the fields, and the traces behind a dev flag. → /impeccable layout, /impeccable distill
2. [P1] The 10 stage tones can't be told apart by colour. Amber-1 vs amber-2 is dE 10.4. Under deuteranopia, Case Analysed vs Failed is dE 10.3 with a luminance ratio of 1.16, so both show as olive. Fraud Detection vs Finalised is 1.72:1. The amber-1, amber-2 and blue-1 fills fail 3:1 against white. Documents Incomplete and Ready for Analysis share one tint. Fix: colour carries the group (system working, officer's turn, done, failed), and a second channel (shape or step number) carries the stage. Cut amber to 2 shades, make Finalised neutral, and darken the light fills. → /impeccable colorize
3. [P1] Warm colours are overloaded and reds mean too many things. Failed, severity High, error and risk High are four near-identical reds, with 7 more red tokens and hot pinks (red-51a, red-47a) for the approval warning. Manual review is shown in red (summary-icon--warning, summary-count). Risk has 4 colour mappings, and the dashboard shows Low in green. Severity and risk share a hue band with workflow amber. Fix: one error red, one risk and severity ramp shifted towards crimson, amber only for "your turn", and `--risk-*` tokens used everywhere. → /impeccable colorize
4. [P1] Tertiary grey text fails contrast. slate-48 to slate-66 sit at 2.4–3.95:1, often at 10–11px: the search placeholder, table headers, footers, detail labels and metric notes. Amber text #c6820a is 3.18:1 on white. ink-soft drops to 4.41:1 on the active case nav. Fix: one muted-text token of 4.5:1 or better on white and paper (around #5b6478 to #66738a), amber text in orange-27, and a 11px minimum. → /impeccable audit, /impeccable typeset
5. [P2] The decision is buried. Approve and reject are at the end of a long page, with all 35 fields first and no next or previous document. Fix: a sticky action bar with the count of fields below threshold and next/previous. → /impeccable layout

## Colour: other improvements
- Severity Low and risk Low fills (#fbe3d5) are 1.23:1 against white. Add an orange-71 border or use an outlined tag.
- Green should mean confirmed only. Nav dots show green for "3 Findings", and every confidence chip is green. Colour only the confidence values below threshold, in amber.
- Cool slate surfaces (slate-97, slate-97a, the "What happens next" card) drift from the warm paper. Use the sand family for sunken surfaces.
- The create-case switch is teal, not steel.
- Token sprawl: 150 colour tokens, 49 near-duplicate pairs (dE < 2), 45 used once, and misleading names (slate-50 is blue, slate-45 is brown). Consolidate to about 30 semantic tokens.
- Self-host IBM Plex Mono, because bank networks may block Google Fonts.
